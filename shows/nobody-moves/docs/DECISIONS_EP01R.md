# Episode 1 remake: decisions D1–D10 (made for engagement)

The decisions behind "Three Feet, Reenacted" (`episodes/ep01r_three_feet_reenacted/`), made on the proposal package before it was merged, applied to every file they touch, then reviewed a third time (34 findings; `ep01r/NOTES.md`, "Review history", round 3). On the Kokoro scratch voices the remake runs **46.9 s in 14 shots** (87 words: 111 words/min overall, 167 while speaking), **3.1 s under 50 s**. At ~150 words/min it would run 50.4 s at 104 words/min overall, 0.4 s over the ceiling: D1's overall target and D2's headroom hold on v4 only if the takes average about 164 words/min or faster while speaking. Paths are relative to `shows/nobody-moves/`; `ep01r/` is `episodes/ep01r_three_feet_reenacted/`. The general rules they produced are in `SERIES.md` ("Reenactments", "3D character references") and the `nobody-moves-*` skills; the voices are in [`AUDIO_ELEVENLABS_V4.md`](AUDIO_ELEVENLABS_V4.md).
