# Audience engagement and version testing

The series has a repeatable viewer action: **watch the evidence, identify the changed object, and give the timestamp**. A viewer should get a satisfying small answer in each episode and a reason to return for the next witness or clue. The public copy must not give away the hidden change in a title, cover, profile, or creator reply before its on-screen payoff.

## Three creative angles

| Version | First impression | Best use | Watch for |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: puzzle-first | "I can solve this" | Launch profiles, Case 001, evidence recaps | Comments naming an object and timestamp; rewatch and saves |
| B: witness characters | "I want to hear this cast" | Confessionals, interview clips, a later cover test | Replies naming a witness, shares, follows after interviews |
| C: case-file voice | "What happened at 3:12?" | Exhibit cards and recap posts | Theories grounded in a specific frame; completion |

Use A first so the accounts teach the viewing game. Test B and C in later posts with similar length and topic. Change one element at a time where practical: caption question *or* cover direction *or* opening hook. Keep episode numbering, the yellow avatar, and the account handle stable during tests. Avoid reposting the identical pilot repeatedly just to compare variants.

## Comment prompts and responses

Primary prompt: `What changed besides Deb? Object + timestamp.`

Secondary prompts for later posts:

- `Which witness should be questioned next, and what would you ask?`
- `Which piece of evidence made you change your theory?`
- `Name one thing the camera shows and one thing a witness only claims.`

Spoiler-safe replies in the show's voice:

- `Noted for the file. Which frame led you there?`
- `The statement is on record. The camera remains unpersuaded.`
- `Interesting theory. What can you point to on screen?`
- `Mr. Basin objects. There is still no judge.`

If someone asks whether this is real: `NOBODY MOVES is a scripted fictional mystery comedy. The visuals are AI-assisted.` If someone identifies an unrevealed clue, acknowledge the observation without confirming the plot answer: `Observation logged. We'll revisit that frame.` Avoid telling them they are wrong when the answer is meant to remain open.

## What to record after the first posts

In a working copy of the registry, record the actual caption/cover version and public URL. After enough views for a meaningful comparison, note the platform's available retention measures, comments that contain an object/timestamp, shares/saves, and follows or subscriptions per view. Those show whether the hook brings viewers in and whether the case makes them participate. Use the next episode or Confessional to test the strongest alternative angle. Do not change all three profiles from a single weak post.
