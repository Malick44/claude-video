1. Get authorized access to the creator's logged-in browser or account owner. Use the owner's email, phone, recovery method, and legal identity where the platform asks; **do not put passwords, codes, birthdays, or private identity details into this repo**.
2. Check the final handle on TikTok, Facebook, and YouTube. 4. Add cross-links only after the target accounts exist. A link field or button may be gated or unavailable by region/account type; record the constraint and use the other native link options when available. Never invent a URL.
5. If the owner authorizes posting, use `POST_001.md` on each platform. Upload the reviewed pilot, choose the platform-specific cover, set the AI or synthetic-media disclosure appropriate to the platform, publish, then add the pinned comment and pin the post where supported. Only after that switch any profile wording from pre-post to live wording.
6. Make a final pass: name and avatar match; every link resolves; the video plays with sound and captions; the thumbnail is readable in the profile grid; fiction/AI disclosure is visible as required; the object-and-timestamp comment prompt appears; no hidden clue is spoiled by art or metadata. Save resulting URLs and status in the registry copy.

## Engagement rules

- The first post should make sense to someone who has never heard of the series: Deb moved three feet; there is a second on-screen change; viewers can inspect it.
- Invite an **object + timestamp** answer. A specific prompt encourages rewatch and gives the comment section a task. Ask for one-sentence theories only when the viewer has enough evidence.
- Reply in the deadpan case-file voice, but do not confirm a correct theory before its episode payoff. Acknowledge observations ("Noted for the file") without falsely presenting fiction as a real crime.
- Pin Episode 1 once it is actually live, then use a later pin for the current episode or a short witness Confessional. Keep the first post accessible as the entry point.
- Do not promise a weekly schedule until one is set. Do not describe a playlist, trailer, pinned post, or outbound link as live until it exists.

## Known owner-dependent inputs

Authentication and verification, the final common handle, any owned website or contact email, whether to publish Case 001 immediately, and any legal/business account fields come from the owner or platform UI. This kit supplies the creative fields without inventing those details.
