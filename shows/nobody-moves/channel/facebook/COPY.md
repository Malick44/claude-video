# Facebook Page copy — three versions

**Version A is the launch choice.** A short Bio plus a fuller About paragraph gives a new visitor the premise and the action to take. B and C are complete alternate positioning. The Page name can stay `NOBODY MOVES` across every version; the alternate subtitles belong in a cover or post, not necessarily the Page name.

## A — puzzle-first (recommended)

Page name:

```text
NOBODY MOVES
```

Short Bio / Intro:

```text
A fictional true-crime parody about lawn ornaments. Watch the evidence. Spot the clue.
```

About / Description:

```text
At No. 7 Birchwood Court, a flamingo moved three feet at 3:12 AM. Every witness is a lawn ornament. NOBODY MOVES is a scripted mystery comedy told as a serious documentary. Each main case file ends with a changed doorbell-camera frame. Watch closely, then comment the object and timestamp. Photoreal visuals and reenactments are AI-assisted.
```

Short tagline for a Page intro graphic or featured post: `Solve the yard, one frame at a time.`

## B — witness characters

Short Bio / Intro:

```text
The lawn ornaments saw everything. Each witness has a story. Scripted comedy series.
```

About / Description:

```text
Garrison the gnome was facing the other way. Lorraine the porch goose says concrete is an alibi. Mr. Basin the birdbath represents himself. When Deb the flamingo moves three feet, every lawn ornament at No. 7 Birchwood Court becomes a witness. NOBODY MOVES is a fictional documentary-style mystery comedy with clues you can inspect. Photoreal visuals and reenactments are AI-assisted.
```

Tagline: `A neighborhood mystery told by the least helpful witnesses in the yard.`

## C — case-file voice

Short Bio / Intro:

```text
Case files from Birchwood Court. Every witness is stationary. Fictional parody.
```

About / Description:

```text
Case 001: a pink flamingo is found three feet from her original position. The evidence: two holes in the lawn and a doorbell-camera frame. The witnesses: lawn ornaments, all claiming they did not move. NOBODY MOVES is a scripted true-crime parody. Inspect each case file, separate what the camera shows from what the witnesses claim, and file your theory in the comments. Photoreal visuals and reenactments are AI-assisted.
```

Tagline: `The evidence moves. The witnesses do not.`

## Search and accessibility text

Use these naturally in posts and Page details where relevant: fictional mystery series; true-crime parody; lawn ornament comedy; garden gnome; flamingo; spot-the-clue. Do not claim Birchwood Court is a real address. Cover alt text: `Case-board artwork for NOBODY MOVES, a fictional lawn-ornament mystery series.`
